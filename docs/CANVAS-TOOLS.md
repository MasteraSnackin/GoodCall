# Canvas editing tools

The editing toolbar sits below **Find a card**. On a narrow screen, scroll the toolbar sideways to reach the remaining controls, or choose **Expand canvas** for more room.

| Tool | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Select | Drag a box across cards or hold Shift, Cmd or Ctrl while clicking to add or remove items from the selection. The count shows the selected items. |
| Pan | Drag the background to move around the board. Cards remain editable. |
| Note | Add a review note in the current view. Type directly into it; move it by its heading. |
| Align | Align two or more unlocked items to the left or top. Space three or more horizontally or vertically using their measured sizes. |
| Lock / Unlock | Keep the selected positions fixed or allow them to move again. Text remains editable. |
| Undo / Redo | Reverse or reapply card and note movement, alignment, Arrange and position locks. |

Drag a selected card or the selection box to move the group. Press Escape to clear a group before editing an individual card or note. Locked items stay in place. Arrow keys move a focused card or group. Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z control layout history while focus is on the canvas; text fields retain their own editing shortcuts.

Layout history holds the latest 50 actions in the current page session. Reloading the page or restoring a workspace clears that history. Saved positions, locks and review notes remain in the workspace. Undo never restores old answer wording, evidence connections, approval state or review-note text. Adding and removing notes are not layout-history actions.

Review notes are working annotations, limited to 4,000 characters each and 500 notes per workspace. They have no evidence handles and are excluded from AI requests, source checks and published advice. They are included in private workspace backups. A note can be removed with its bin button; the existing workspace recovery flow retains the previous saved workspace.

**Arrange** organises unlocked evidence cards into columns and leaves review notes where you placed them. Automatic card placement reserves space around existing review notes. Search and Focus connections continue to operate on evidence cards; review notes are hidden while Focus connections is active.


## Decision tools

Answer cards now show a separate evidence-readiness button: **Checks needed**, **Evidence changed**, **Ready for review**, or **Checks passed**. Open it to see every current blocker and the full attached sources. Changed product records are displayed alongside the sources saved on the answer. These checks do not approve an answer, and a resolved report does not clear unsupported wording.

To compare products, add them from **Maya’s knowledge**, select two or three product cards on the canvas, then choose **Compare**. The panel compares recorded facts and Maya’s notes. Confirm **Buy together** or **Choose one alternative**, and mark each product as already owned or a new purchase. An optional confirmed budget shows the amount remaining or the overspend for each separate option. Unknown roles, invalid prices and missing records prevent a total. Only one unit per new purchase is supported. Comparison choices are temporary and clear when the panel closes; they never change a draft or create a basket.

## Follow-ups and decision sections

Select a question and open **Follow-ups & sections** to write and track a clarification. There is one editable follow-up per question. **Copy question** copies text for you to paste; it does not send a message. Mark **Context received** to update the tracker, then use **Update question context** to record the actual reply through the existing review flow. Editing a saved follow-up returns it to **Waiting for reply**. Saved follow-ups appear on their question cards.

Select one or more evidence cards, open **Follow-ups & sections**, name the decision and choose **Create section from selection**. A labelled outline follows the member cards, showing their count and any blocked answers or waiting replies. Sections can be renamed, focused, given a replacement selection or removed. Removing a section keeps all its cards and connections. Private review notes remain outside section membership.

Follow-ups and section membership are saved locally and included in workspace backups. Older version 1 backups remain supported. The private tracking fields are excluded from AI requests and published advice. Layout Undo/Redo continues to cover card positions and locks; it does not change follow-ups or section membership.
